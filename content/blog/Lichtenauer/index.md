## About
I have always loved 3D 1vMany fighting games. Many of my favorite games come from studios like Sloclap, with standout titles such as *Absolver* and *Sifu*; Sucker Punch Productions, with the *Ghost* series (*Ghost of Tsushima* and *Ghost of Yōtei*); and Ubisoft Montréal, with *For Honor*.

I am also a practitioner of HEMA (Historical European Martial Arts) and have spent a long time studying the KDF tradition of fencing. My passion for martial arts, combined with my love for combat design, led me to create *Lichtenauer*, a game that puts you in the shoes of the historical figure Johannes Lichtenauer, the legendary grandmaster associated with the German martial arts tradition that flourished from the 14th through 16th centuries.

Everything about *Lichtenauer* is designed around one central fantasy: 
# **You are the Grandmaster.**
